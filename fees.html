How you pay

Medicare card holders

  • Pay one amount: facility fee + Medicare charges
  • Get an instant refund of the Medicare part only

No Medicare card

  • Pay one amount on discharge: facility fee + Medicare-equivalent charges
  • Pathology is billed separately by ACL

What Medicare charges cover

  • Doctor consultation
  • Any procedures performed

Pathology

  • Bloods, swabs and urine tests
  • Collected on site by your treating team
  • Processed in Australian clinical labs
  • Medicare card holders: bulk billed, no pathology charge
  • No Medicare card: extra charge, paid separately
  • Australian Clinical Labs (ACL) sends you its own invoice after your test
